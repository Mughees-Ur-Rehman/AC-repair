Should You Repair or Replace Your AC in Las Vegas?

When your AC breaks down, the big question is whether to fix it or buy a new one. Here are the factors that help you decide.

VVegas Cool AC RepairOctober 20263 min read

A failing air conditioner puts you in a tough spot. A repair may be the right call, but if the system is old or keeps breaking, replacement can make more sense.

There is no single rule that fits every home, but these factors will help you think it through before you talk to a technician.

Consider the Age of Your System

Air conditioners often last roughly 10 to 15 years, though the real figure depends on the equipment, how it was installed and how well it has been maintained. Constant heavy use in the Las Vegas summer can wear a system down faster.

A newer system with a single failed part is usually a good candidate for repair. An older system with repeated problems is a different story.

Look at the Repair History

Think about how often you have called for repairs lately. One breakdown is common. Several in a short period can mean parts are wearing out across the system, and each repair may only buy you a little time.

Compare Repair Cost with Replacement

Ask for a clear estimate for the repair, and compare it with the cost of a new system. A commonly used rule of thumb is that if a repair costs a large share of a replacement, replacement is worth serious thought, especially on an older unit.

A good technician will explain both options without pushing you toward either.

Think About Refrigerant and Efficiency

Older systems may use R-22 refrigerant, which is no longer produced in the United States, and can be costly to service. Newer systems are also generally more efficient, which can lower your cooling bills over time.

If your cooling costs have been climbing, ask whether an efficiency upgrade could help.

When Repair Makes Sense

  • The system is relatively new
  • The problem is a single, well-defined part
  • The system has been well maintained
  • The repair cost is small compared with replacement

When Replacement Makes Sense

  • The system is near or past the end of its expected life
  • Repairs are becoming frequent
  • The unit uses an older refrigerant that is hard to service
  • Your cooling costs keep rising

If you decide to replace, our AC installation service can help you choose a system that suits your home.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does an AC last in Las Vegas? Many systems last around 10 to 15 years, but heavy summer use and maintenance habits affect lifespan. A technician can tell you more about your specific unit.

2. Is it worth repairing an old AC? It can be if the problem is small and the repair cost is low. If the unit is old and failing often, replacement may be the better value.

3. What is R-22 refrigerant? R-22 is an older refrigerant that is no longer produced in the United States. Systems that use it can be costly to service.

4. Can a technician help me decide? Yes. We can inspect your system, explain the repair and replacement options and let you make the final decision.
