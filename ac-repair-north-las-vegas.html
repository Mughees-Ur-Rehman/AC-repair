The hot desert climate of North Las Vegas contributes to several common AC problems. Understanding these issues can help homeowners take preventative measures and seek timely repairs.

Frozen evaporator coils

High temperatures and low airflow can cause evaporator coils to freeze, leading to system failures. We check airflow and refrigerant levels during maintenance to prevent freezing and ensure optimal operation.

02

Leaking refrigerant

Age and wear on AC systems can lead to refrigerant leaks, compromising efficiency and cooling performance. Our technicians are trained to locate and repair leaks, ensuring your system operates efficiently.

03

Thermostat issues

Thermostat malfunctions can cause improper temperature regulation, leading to discomfort. We offer thorough diagnostics to identify and resolve thermostat problems swiftly.

04

Electrical failures

Electrical issues can arise from wear and tear or faulty components, leading to system shutdowns. Our team conducts comprehensive electrical inspections to ensure all components are functioning correctly.

Warning signs

Signs You Need AC Repair in North Las Vegas

  • AC not cooling effectivelyIf your AC is running but not cooling your home, it may indicate a refrigerant leak or a malfunctioning compressor, both requiring immediate attention.
  • Strange noises from the unitUnusual sounds like banging or hissing can signal mechanical issues that need professional assessment to avoid further damage.
  • Frequent cycling on and offIf your AC constantly turns on and off, it could be a sign of thermostat issues or airflow problems that need to be addressed.
  • Increased energy billsA sudden spike in energy costs can indicate that your AC unit is working harder than it should, often due to inefficiency or malfunction.
  • Unpleasant odors from ventsFoul smells can suggest mold or electrical issues within your AC system, necessitating immediate professional evaluation.
