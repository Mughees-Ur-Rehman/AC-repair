Common AC Repair Problems in Henderson

Henderson's desert climate leads to specific AC issues that homeowners frequently encounter. Understanding these common problems can help you recognize when to seek Henderson AC repair services.

01

Frozen evaporator coils

In Henderson's dry heat, low airflow caused by dirty filters or blocked ducts can lead to evaporator coils freezing. We perform thorough inspections and cleaning to restore airflow and prevent future freezing.

02

Leaking refrigerant

Refrigerant leaks can occur due to wear and tear on older systems, especially in the harsh Henderson climate. Our technicians quickly locate and repair leaks, ensuring optimal refrigerant levels for efficient cooling.

03

Thermostat issues

Thermostats can malfunction or become miscalibrated, leading to improper temperature regulation in homes. We assess and recalibrate thermostats to ensure accurate temperature control and comfort.

04

Clogged condensate drain

Dust and debris can block the condensate drain, leading to water damage and system inefficiencies. We clean and maintain condensate drains regularly to prevent clogs and ensure proper drainage.

Warning signs

Signs You Need AC Repair in Henderson

  • AC not cooling properlyIf your AC system is running but not cooling effectively, it could indicate a refrigerant leak or compressor issue that needs immediate attention.
  • Strange noises from the unitUnusual sounds like banging or hissing can signal mechanical problems or loose parts, which should be addressed promptly.
  • Increased energy billsA sudden spike in your energy bills may suggest that your AC is working harder than it should due to inefficiencies, often requiring repairs.
  • Frequent cycling on and offIf your AC unit frequently turns on and off, it could indicate issues with the thermostat or electrical components that need to be checked.
  • Unpleasant odors from the ventsFoul smells can indicate mold growth or burnt components within the AC system, necessitating immediate inspection and repair.
