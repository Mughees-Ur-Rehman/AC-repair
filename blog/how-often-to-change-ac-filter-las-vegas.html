How Often Should You Change Your AC Filter in Las Vegas?

A clean filter is one of the easiest ways to protect your AC. Here is how often to change it in Las Vegas and what to look for.

VVegas Cool AC RepairOctober 20263 min read

The air filter is a small part that does a big job. It traps dust before it reaches your system and helps keep air moving freely. When it clogs, your AC has to work harder to cool your home.

A General Rule

Many homeowners replace or clean filters every 1 to 3 months, and more often during peak summer use. The right schedule depends on your filter type, so check the manufacturer's recommendation as well.

Why Las Vegas Homes May Need Changes More Often

  • Desert dust. Fine dust gets into homes and collects on filters.
  • Long cooling season. Your system runs for many hours, so the filter handles a lot of air.
  • Pets. Pet hair and dander fill filters faster.
  • Construction nearby. Dust from building work can load up a filter quickly.

How to Check Your Filter

Slide the filter out and hold it up to a light. If you cannot see light through it, or it looks gray and clogged, it is time for a new one. Make sure the arrow on the frame points toward the air handler when you put the new filter in.

What a Dirty Filter Can Cause

  • Weaker airflow and uneven cooling
  • Higher energy use because the system works harder
  • A frozen evaporator coil
  • More dust circulating in your home

Regular checks are part of our AC maintenance service, and if airflow problems have already caused trouble, our AC repair team can help.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How often should I change my AC filter? Many homes need a new or cleaned filter every 1 to 3 months. Check it more often during heavy summer use or if you have pets.

2. What happens if I never change my filter? Airflow drops, the system works harder and problems such as a frozen coil become more likely.

3. Which filter should I buy? Use the size printed on your current filter, and follow your system's recommendations for filter type. Very restrictive filters are not right for every system.

4. Can I clean my filter instead of replacing it? Only if it is a reusable washable type. Disposable filters should be replaced.
