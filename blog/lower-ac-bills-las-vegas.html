How to Lower Your AC Bills in Las Vegas

Cooling costs add up quickly in the desert. These practical steps can help you use less energy without giving up comfort.

VVegas Cool AC RepairOctober 20263 min read

In Las Vegas, cooling is a major part of the household energy bill. You cannot change the weather, but you can make sure your system is not wasting energy.

These steps range from free habits to repairs that pay off over time.

Set Your Thermostat Wisely

Many energy agencies suggest setting the thermostat around 78°F when you are home and a little higher when you are away. Avoid dramatic changes, since the system may run for a long time to recover.

A programmable or smart thermostat can handle this automatically. Our thermostat installation service includes setup and a walkthrough.

Keep Filters and Coils Clean

A dirty filter restricts airflow and forces your system to work harder. Replace filters regularly and keep the area around the outdoor unit clear of debris.

Check Your Ducts for Leaks

Ducts that run through a hot attic can lose cooled air through gaps and loose joints. If some rooms never feel cool, leaking ducts may be part of the reason. Our air duct repair service inspects and seals ductwork.

Reduce Heat Coming into the Home

  • Close blinds and curtains on sun-facing windows during the day
  • Run heat-producing appliances, such as ovens, in the evening when possible
  • Use ceiling fans to help air feel cooler in occupied rooms
  • Check door and window seals for gaps

Schedule Regular Maintenance

A system that is cleaned, inspected and adjusted tends to run more efficiently, and small faults are caught before they waste energy. See our AC maintenance service for what a visit includes.

If your bills jumped suddenly with no change in habits, your AC may have a problem such as low refrigerant or a failing part, and an AC repair visit can find it.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What temperature should I set my AC in Las Vegas? Many energy agencies suggest around 78°F when you are home. Choose a setting that is comfortable for you and avoid large swings.

2. Why is my AC bill so high? Common causes include dirty filters, leaking ducts, an inefficient or failing system and long hours of use during extreme heat.

3. Does a smart thermostat save money? It can help by following schedules that match when you are home, though savings depend on how you use it.

4. Can duct repair lower my cooling bill? Sealing leaks means more of the cooled air reaches your rooms, which can help your system run less.
