Common AC Problems We Solve

We tackle a range of common AC issues faced by homeowners in Las Vegas.

01

AC not cooling effectively

This is often due to refrigerant leaks or compressor issues. Our technicians will diagnose the problem and perform necessary repairs to restore cooling.

02

Frozen evaporator coils

Frozen coils can indicate airflow issues or low refrigerant levels. We quickly identify the cause and provide solutions to prevent future occurrences.

03

Thermostat malfunctions

A malfunctioning thermostat can lead to inconsistent temperatures. We check and recalibrate or replace your thermostat as needed.

04

Leaking refrigerant

Refrigerant leaks can affect system efficiency and performance. We locate and repair leaks, then recharge your system for optimal operation.
